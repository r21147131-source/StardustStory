## Description
The history of Israel and Palestine, from the Bronze Age to 1948, told as the story of one narrow strip of land that every empire wanted. Egypt, Assyria, Babylon, Persia, Rome, the caliphs, the Crusaders, the Mamluks, the Ottomans and Britain all crossed it. Two peoples stayed.

This episode does not take a side. It follows the sources: Egyptian campaign records, the Merneptah Stele, the Tel Dan inscription, Assyrian palace reliefs, the Cyrus Cylinder, Roman monuments, the Balfour Declaration and UN Resolution 181. Where historians disagree, the video says so.

Pantheon is a narrated history series. Episode 5 covers about 3,500 years in 16 minutes.
