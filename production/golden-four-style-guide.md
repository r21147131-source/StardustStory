# Golden Four style guide (from reference video y4YfRtfJoAQ — "Golden Four · Ep 2", Joseph Quinn, ~9:10)
Source: vidIQ video analysis. Apply the same style to the Stardust Story (Deborah Ann Woll) cut.

## Picture
- Mostly REAL footage: TV clips, official trailer clips, cast interview / BTS clips, plus official poster / key art.
- Cutout portraits of named people (feathered mask, drop shadow) over a dark blurred/gradient studio backdrop.
- Stills (posters, comic covers) get constant slow push-in / pull-out (Ken Burns).
- Footage keeps its native 2.35-2.39:1 letterbox inside a 16:9 canvas. Cinematic grade: crushed blacks, warm highlights, light film grain.

## Labels (gold serif, all caps, tracked out)
- Series tag top-left for the first ~11 s: `GOLDEN FOUR · EP n` (adapt: `STARDUST STORY`).
- Person lower-third, bottom-left: NAME (large) over role (`ACTOR`, `DIRECTOR`) in smaller gold.
- Film/project lower-third, bottom-left: TITLE over release year.
- Callout card bottom-right (PiP framed headshot + `GOLDEN FOUR · EP 1 / name`) for references to earlier episodes.
- Chapter cards: pure black, `CHAPTER IV` small tracked gold, thin gold rule, `TITLE OF CHAPTER` larger. One every 45-90 s, ~1 s long.
- Ending quote card, centered on black, serif.

## Cutting
- ~90% hard cuts. Cross-dissolve / light-leak only when moving from interview/BTS into cinematic clips.
- ASL 2.5-4 s under narration, 1-2 s in montage peaks.
- Cut to black for chapter cards.

## Audio
- Low cinematic drone/synth pad throughout, risers on reveals; sub-bass hit + whoosh on each chapter card.
- Narration dry and centered; film audio kept low or muted under VO.

## Structure
- Hook recaps previous episode, sets contrast. Chapters. Summary, next-episode teaser (title card style), CTA, then philosophical quote on black.
