### [1:45 — THE BORING BUSINESS]

### [2:35 — THE PROBLEM]
Imagine putting on a leather work boot in a New England winter.

The sole can have enormous traction.
But there is still one weak point: the connection between the two.
Traditional boots were assembled through stitching.

So the Swartz family started looking for another solution.

Then came another innovation: silicone-treated leather.

The goal was simple.
