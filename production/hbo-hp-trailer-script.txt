HBO's Harry Potter trailer broke every record

277 million views in 48 hours. HBO's Harry Potter trailer is now the most-watched in HBO history, beating the old record by more than double.
The trailer opens with first-years arriving at Hogwarts, McGonagall leading them into the Great Hall, then the Sorting Ceremony, with Harry placed in Gryffindor.
There's the first Quidditch match. There's the mountain troll in the bathroom, with the trio facing it together.
Dominic McLaughlin is Harry, with Arabella Stanton as Hermione and Alastair Stout as Ron. John Lithgow is Dumbledore, Paapa Essiedu is Snape, and Nick Frost is Hagrid.
It ends on Ron's question: "Are you really Harry Potter?"
Eight episodes this season, one book per season after that. Season 2 is already in the works. It premieres Christmas 2026, only on HBO Max.
