# Real Riquelme footage needed

Drop clips (mp4/mov/mkv/webm) into `public/riquelme-footage/<SCENE>/` and rerun `python3 production/riquelme/build.py`.
A scene with your clips uses only those; scenes without keep the stock B-roll.

| S3 | Barcelona (~1:40) | Riquelme in Barcelona shirt 2002-03 |
| S4 | Villarreal (~1:55) | Passes, free kicks, 2004-06 |
| S5 | Champions League (~2:40) | Inter QF, Arsenal semi, the Lehmann penalty save |
