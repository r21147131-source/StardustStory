# Stardust Story — Netflix-style edit plan (Deborah Ann Woll / Karen Page)

Format: 1920x1080, 24 fps, ~13:22, voiceover = the 7 mp3s in `public/stardust-story/`.
Look: letterboxed 2.39:1 grade, slow push-ins, film grain, low music bed (ducked under VO), red/black title cards.

## Label system (burned in, lower-third style)
- **NAME** — person's name + role, first mention only (white on black bar, 3s).
- **FILM/SHOW** — title + year + network (top-left, 3s, small caps).
- **CITY** — Brooklyn, New York · Los Angeles · London · Hell's Kitchen (bottom-right, typewriter reveal).
- **MONEY/STAT** — big number card (e.g. "7 seasons", "10 years", "1999", "2026") with count-up.
- **QUOTE** — full-screen serif quote card for the 3 direct quotes (04:09, 05:18, 11:36).

## Transitions (editor's rules)
- Cuts on VO sentence ends; match-cut on same-subject images.
- Cross-dissolve (12 f) between biography stills; dip-to-black between acts.
- Whip/glitch only for Netflix-era -> Marvel Studios era jumps (08:11, 09:06).
- J-cuts: film audio swells 0.5s before the picture it belongs to; L-cuts for interview sound bites.
