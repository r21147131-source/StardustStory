## AI VIDEO SHOTS (9 script shots + 2 archival-fallback shots for S04/S13)

### Shot 1 — Night sky meteor streak (Cold Open)
**Prompt:**
Wide static shot of an ancient tundra landscape at night under a vast starfield. A brilliant meteor streaks diagonally across the sky, breaking into two smaller fragments as it burns, casting a brief blue-white glow across the snow and rock below. Silhouettes of Ice Age animals — a mammoth herd, a dire wolf — pause and lift their heads toward the light. Slow, deliberate push-in camera movement. Naturalistic lighting except for the meteor's glow. Awed, ominous mood.
**Ratio:** 16:9 · **Duration:** 8–10s · **Model:** Veo 3.1 Quality (or Higgsfield `seedance_2_5` if using Higgsfield later)

### Shot 2 — Ice Age hunters with mammoth herd (Part One)
**Prompt:**
Dawn on an open tundra steppe. A small band of five Ice Age hunters in fur and leather clothing, carrying stone-tipped spears, crouches at the tree line watching a distant woolly mammoth herd graze in golden morning light. Breath visible in the cold air. Wide establishing shot, slow horizontal pan revealing the herd. Documentary-style naturalistic lighting, muted cool-to-warm gradient sky. Calm, purposeful mood — humans coexisting with giants.
**Ratio:** 16:9 · **Duration:** 8s · **Model:** Veo 3.1 Quality

### Shot 3 — Impact event sequence (Part Two)
**Prompt:**
A fragmented comet enters Earth's atmosphere at high speed, breaking into a swarm of glowing fragments streaking across a dark sky over a northern ice-sheet landscape. Cut to a massive airburst — a blinding white-orange fireball expanding above the horizon, followed by a visible shockwave flattening trees and throwing up snow and debris in a widening ring. Camera holds on a wide landscape shot, ground trembling. Extreme scale, terrifying, sublime. No text overlays.
**Ratio:** 16:9 · **Duration:** 8–10s · **Model:** Veo 3.1 Quality

### Shot 4 — Post-impact devastated landscape (Part Two/Three)
**Prompt:**
The same tundra landscape now scorched and silent. Blackened, fallen trees, smoldering ground, ash drifting through the air like snow. Thick gray smoke on the horizon partially obscuring a dim red sun. The carcass of a mammoth lies half-buried in ash in the middle distance. Slow drone-style push forward over the wreckage. Somber, funereal mood, desaturated color grade with ember-orange accents.
**Ratio:** 16:9 · **Duration:** 8s · **Model:** Veo 3.1 Quality

### Shot 5 — Environmental transformation montage (Part Four)
**Needs ~36s of screen time — generate as 4 separate sub-clips, not one continuous shot** (this is the pattern that worked well for Shots 1/3/8: several short clips cut together beat a single stretched-out generation, and Veo tops out around 8-10s per clip anyway).

**5a — Forest withers:** Static wide shot of a green tundra forest under overcast sky. Over the shot, needles brown and branches go bare and brittle, frost creeping in from the edges. Cool blue-gray color grade, very slow zoom in. Mood: quiet, relentless decline.
**5b — Glacier advances:** Wide shot of a mountain valley with a distant glacier. The ice visibly thickens and pushes forward into the valley floor, swallowing the green at its edges. Same cool color grade, slow zoom in.
**5c — Herd declines:** A mammoth herd trudging through deepening snow on an open plain, moving slower, several animals falling behind and struggling. Wide tracking shot alongside the herd, overcast light.
**5d — Camp shrinks:** A small cluster of hide tents on a windswept ridge, shot from the same angle at two implied points in time — first several tents with people moving between them, then fewer tents, more huddled, smoke thin, wind stronger. Static wide shot, slow zoom in.

*Note: the "YEAR 0 / YEAR 50" four-panel split-screen comparison image previewed earlier in this session (forest→bare branches, valley→glacier, herd→one mammoth down, tents→storm) is close to a ready-made treatment for this shot — if that generation finished, it may already cover 5a-5d in one asset. Worth checking before generating fresh.*
**Ratio:** 16:9 · **Duration:** ~8-9s each · **Model:** Veo 3.1 Quality

### Shot 6 — Desperate hunter group in changing landscape (Part Five)
**Needs ~25s of screen time. One clip already received** (`S18a-woman-looking-at-child.mp4` — woman crouched with child, three hunters walking away in the background, empty snowy plain). **Generate 2-3 more sub-clips of the same family/group** to cut together with it:

**6b — Establishing wide:** The same small group of Ice Age humans — a hunter, an elder, the woman and child — walking single-file across an empty, frost-covered plain where a herd used to graze. Overcast light, harsh wind moving fur cloaks and hair. Wide shot, slow tracking alongside them. Weary, exposed mood.
**6c — Elder looks back:** Close-up on the elder hunter, stopping and turning to look back at the empty horizon behind the group — where the herd should be. His breath visible in the cold. Static shot, slow push-in on his face.
**6d — Child's face:** Close-up on the wrapped child's face, looking up at the mother, cold and quiet. Slow, still, minimal movement — the emotional beat to intercut with the wider shots.

**Ratio:** 16:9 · **Duration:** ~6-8s each · **Model:** Veo 3.1 Quality

### Shot 8 — Modern landscape fade (Closing)

A wide shot of a present-day landscape — rolling green hills transitioning into a distant modern city skyline at dusk, lights beginning to glow in windows. Camera holds static, very slow dolly back. The scene gradually desaturates and fades to black over the final seconds. Reflective, time-passing mood, warm-to-cool color transition.

**9b — Greek:** A Greek man and woman in draped chitons on a hillside, watching a distant deluge sweep across a valley below, water reflecting a strange light in the sky. Marble-white and sea-blue palette. Slow push-in.
**9c — Hindu:** A figure in simple dhoti-style dress on a riverbank at dusk, watching both fire in the sky and rising water simultaneously, hands raised. Saffron and firelight palette. Slow push-in.
**9d — Maya:** A Maya figure in woven textile and jade ornament, standing before a stone structure, looking up as the sky darkens unnaturally. Jade-green and stone-gray palette. Slow push-in.
**9e — Indigenous American:** A figure in hide and fur clothing on an open plain, looking up as ice/snow begins falling from a darkening sky, sheltering a child. Ochre and frost-blue palette. Slow push-in.

**Ratio:** 16:9 · **Duration:** ~6s each · **Model:** Veo 3.1 Quality

## MOTION GRAPHICS (9) — spec sheet, not AI video

These are data/text animations best built in After Effects, Premiere Motion
Graphics templates, or a code-based tool (Remotion — this repo already has the
dependency) rather than generative video, since they need precise, legible
data and typography.

1. **Timeline axis** — horizontal line, tick marks, clock hand spinning backward to a highlighted "12,800 BCE" marker. Glacial-blue line on ash-gray background.
2. **Ice sheet extent map** — world/N-hemisphere map, ice sheet extent in white/blue overlay, green habitable zones fading in, population dots pulsing in concentration areas.
3. **Extinction rate chart** — animated line/bar chart, species count dropping sharply at the 12,800 BCE mark; silhouette icons (mammoth, ground sloth, saber-tooth, dire wolf) fading out as their genus is lost.
4. **Climate temperature plunge** — Greenland ice-core-style line graph, temperature dropping ~10°C over a compressed timeframe, Younger Dryas period highlighted as a shaded band.
5. **AMOC disruption diagram** — simplified Atlantic map with ocean conveyor-belt arrows; freshwater influx shown as a blue wedge cutting the circulation, arrows stalling.
6. **Population density map** — pre/post-impact split-screen or crossfade map, dot-density regions shrinking in the north.
7. **Global myth text montage** — Sumerian, Greek, Hindu, Maya, Indigenous American: one-line myth captions typing on/off over faint cultural motif backgrounds (cuneiform texture, Greek pottery pattern, etc.), terracotta/parchment palette.
8. **Neolithic transition timeline** — horizontal timeline bar transitioning hunter-gatherer icon set into agricultural/settlement icon set across the Younger Dryas recovery period.
9. **Source/citation crawl** — simple end-credit scroll, matches Pantheon logo typography.

**Palette for all graphics:** glacial blues, ash grays, terracotta — matching the episode's visual style direction. Keep typography and iconography consistent across all nine for a single coherent "Pantheon" graphics system.
