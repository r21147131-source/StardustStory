## Motion graphic briefs (10 graphics)

All ten are 2D and fit either tool. Use whichever won your Remotion versus Blender test; Remotion is the natural fit if it is still undecided. Palette: obsidian #0A0908, gold #C9A84C, light gold #E8C97A, ember #C45A20, parchment #F0E8D0. Type: Cinzel for labels, Crimson Text italic for dates and captions.

### MG1: Fracture and Rise (Sections 2, 1)

A flat, ink-and-gold Nile valley map in the Pantheon palette. Four phases: (1) Egypt gold in the north, Kush outlined in the south; (2) Egypt cracks into rival realms, the gold fill splinters, date c. 1070 BCE; (3) Kush warms and widens while Sais, Hermopolis, Herakleopolis, Memphis and Thebes sit dim; (4) the names ALARA, KASHTA carve themselves in stone and an arrow runs from Napata to Upper Egypt.

*About 30 seconds total across phases. No more than five text elements on screen at once.*

### MG2: The Campaign (Section 3)

Same map, closer. Coalition markers join Sais and an arrow runs south; Memphis flips color; Herakleopolis is ringed. Then Piye's army arrow runs from Napata north, and Piye's own arrow follows. Hermopolis flips back to Piye's color after the surrender; Memphis flips last.

*About 25 seconds. Keep arrow paths along the river only.*

### MG3: Harbor Assault (Section 3)

Top-down schematic of Memphis. Show a siege ramp and tower option outlined and struck through, then a swarm of small ship icons sweeping onto the harbor quay, the walls turning gold from the water side.

*About 10 seconds. This is a schematic, not a battle animation.*

### MG4: The Empire Breathes (Sections 4, 5)

The Nile glows gold from the Delta to deep in Kush with the label the 25th Dynasty and date c. 690 BCE. Later it contracts: an army arrow runs south along the Nile, Napata dims and the glow settles further upriver.

*About 10 seconds expansion, about 8 seconds contraction. Two separate shots from the same file.*

### MG5: Assyria and the Timeline (Sections 4, 5)

Assyria spreads west across the map like ink toward the Nile. Later, a timeline strip: 671 stamped, Esarhaddon's route ends mid-way, Ashurbanipal enters, the strip ends on 664. Only dates used in the script go on screen.

*About 10 seconds ink spread, about 15 seconds timeline.*

### MG6: The Erasure (Sections 1, 5, 7)

A wall of golden cartouches on dark stone. In the cold open it forms a title card around a hollow oval. In Section 5 the cartouches vanish one by one leaving hollow ovals. In Section 7 the hollows refill in gold reading PIYE, TAHARQA, TANTAMANI, AMANIRENAS. The same asset, three payoffs; render the stone texture once and reuse.

*Title card 4 seconds, erasure 8 seconds, refill 8 seconds.*

### MG7: Kandake (Section 6)

Pure typography on black. CANDACE appears as if it were a name, a line strikes it, KANDAKE replaces it with the caption a title, not a name, then AMANIRENAS carves itself into stone.

*About 12 seconds. Cinzel, gold.*

### MG8: Rome and Kush (Section 6)

Map of Roman Egypt and Kush. Kush armies push north, Roman counter-arrow strikes Napata, envoy ships cross to Samos. Resulting border line shows Kush independent and a tribute mark struck out. No year overlay unless you confirm 21 BCE against a source.

*About 15 seconds.*

### MG9: The King List (Section 7)

A scroll of royal names scrolls vertically. The 25th Dynasty names fill in; Piye's line stays a blank slot with a faint outline.

*About 8 seconds.*

### MG10: Meroitic Script (Section 7)

Rows of Meroitic characters on dark stone. Royal names light fully in gold; long sentences glow faintly then go dark. Use a Meroitic font or hand-traced glyphs; never invent a transliteration on screen.

*About 12 seconds.*
