## Archival licence key

I did not open individual Commons file pages. Each archival beat links a Commons search plus a Wikipedia page or direct link where one exists. Pick a file, then confirm its licence on the file page. Flat reproductions of pre-20th-century paintings, manuscripts, and coins are normally public domain; modern photographs of ruins, artifacts, and museum objects can carry the photographer's own licence. Credit every file in the video description. Two beats (B005, B068) deliberately reuse or echo Episode 4 source material as a visual callback — check `public/pantheon-ep4-archival/` first before sourcing fresh images for those two.

Palette: near-black warm charcoal #15110D, imperial crimson #7A2634, bronze-gold #B08D57, faded gold #D9BF8C, ash parchment #E8E1D3. Type: Cinzel for labels, Crimson Text italic for dates and captions — same type pairing as Episode 4, different palette.

### MG-TITLE (beats B006)

**[B006]** Cold-open title card. Phase 1: the words THE LAST ROMAN carve themselves into a weathered marble slab in gold, cracks already running through the stone. Phase 2: the crack widens and a fragment falls away, leaving the title legible but visibly broken. About 6 seconds.

### MG1 (beats B009)

**[B009]** Roads and Rule. A flat ink-and-gold map of the Mediterranean world. Phase 1: a single road network lights up gold from Britain to the Euphrates. Phase 2: coin icons pulse at major ports from Spain to Syria. Phase 3: caption ROMA AETERNA fades in beneath. About 12 seconds.

### MG2 (beats B013, B014)

**[B013]** The Empire Splits. Same map as MG1, now dividing down a vertical seam. Phase 1: the gold fill cracks into two separate fields, East and West. Phase 2: a small walled-city icon and the label CONSTANTINOPLE appear in the east, glowing brighter. Phase 3: the western field dims to a duller bronze. About 10 seconds.

**[B014]** Continuation of MG2, phase 4: both halves are labeled ROMA, then the western label flickers and fades while the eastern one holds steady. About 5 seconds.

### MG3 (beats B020, B021)

**[B020]** Crossing the River. Danube map close-up. Phase 1: a wave of small refugee-wagon icons crosses the river into Roman territory. Phase 2: the icons flip from pale (refugee) to a weapon icon (armed), rippling outward. About 8 seconds.

**[B021]** Continuation. Phase 3: the converted icons mass into a single army marker south of the river. Phase 4: date stamp 378 CE appears beside it. About 6 seconds.

### MG4 (beats B025)

**[B025]** The Toll of Adrianople. Stat card on dark ground: TWO-THIRDS of the Eastern field army, in one afternoon. Then: the first Roman emperor killed in battle by a foreign enemy in over two hundred years. About 8 seconds.

### MG5 (beats B027, B030)

**[B027]** Three Marches, One Sack. Balkans/Italy map. Phase 1: an army icon wanders through the Balkans and Greece over several loops, a faint trail marking two decades of movement. About 8 seconds.

**[B030]** Continuation. Phase 2: the first three march-lines toward Rome fade out one by one (dotted, failing). Phase 3: a fourth line runs solid and unbroken to the city, and the date 410 CE stamps in large. About 6 seconds.

### MG6 (beats B045)

**[B045]** The Hand That Was Cut Off. Phase 1: quote card, gold serif text on dark marble, the courtier's line appears word by word: ...with your right hand, you have cut off your left. Phase 2: the quote dissolves; new text fades in beneath it: WITHIN A YEAR, THE EMPEROR TOO WAS DEAD, KILLED BY AETIUS'S FORMER BODYGUARDS. About 11 seconds total.

### MG7 (beats B050)

**[B050]** Vandalism. Typography reveal on dark stone: VANDAL carves itself in gold. A line strikes beneath it and VANDALISM grows out from the same letters, larger, with the caption 'a word that outlived the kingdom.' About 10 seconds.

### MG8 (beats B052)

**[B052]** Nine Emperors, Twenty-One Years. A vertical ticker of nine small coin-portrait medallions drops down one by one, each stamped with a reign-length of months or a couple of years; several are struck through with a red/iron mark as they land. About 14 seconds.

### MG9 (beats B058, B059)

**[B058]** 476. Map: a small regalia-icon travels by ship from Ravenna/Rome to Constantinople across the map. The western half of the earlier split-map (from MG2) goes fully dark. About 8 seconds.

**[B059]** Continuation. Big date card on black: 476 CE — THE WESTERN ROMAN EMPIRE ENDS. About 5 seconds.

### MG10 (beats B061, B063, B065)

**[B061]** What Outlived the Throne, phase 1: on black, the word ENDING appears in large gold type, then a crack splits it and the word reassembles, slightly altered, as CONTINUES. About 6 seconds.

**[B063]** Phase 2: the western map from MG2/MG9 relights, now divided into small successor kingdoms, each still faintly gold at the edges. Beneath it, the word LATIN splits and regrows into FRANÇAIS, ESPAÑOL, ITALIANO branching outward like roots. About 12 seconds.

**[B065]** Phase 3: a single gold crown icon on black shatters into a dozen smaller crown icons that scatter outward and settle, each still faintly glowing gold. About 8 seconds.

### MG11 (beats B066, B067)

**[B066]** Caesar's Long Shadow. Typography on dark stone, modeled on Episode 4's Kandake treatment: CAESAR carves itself in gold, then splits and regrows into KAISER and TSAR branching to either side. About 10 seconds.

**[B067]** Continuation. Caption fades in beneath the three words: IT HAD NOT DIED. IT HAD STOPPED BELONGING TO ANY ONE THRONE. About 6 seconds.

### MG12 (beats B069)

**[B069]** Closing thesis card. On black, gold serif text builds line by line: EVERYONE KEPT THE NAME. / ALMOST NO ONE KEPT / THE THING THE NAME ONCE MEANT. About 8 seconds.
